# Vacation and Leave Policy

## Annual vacation
Full-time employees at Northwind Corp get 15 vacation days per year. Part-time employees get vacation days in proportion to their hours.

## Carry-over
Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused vacation days into the next calendar year. Any days above that limit expire on December 31.

## Parental leave
New parents, including adoptive parents, receive 12 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The leave must be taken within the first year after the birth or adoption.

## Requesting time off
Vacation requests of more than 3 days in a row must be submitted to your manager at least 2 weeks in advance through the HR portal.
