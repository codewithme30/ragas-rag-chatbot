# Benefits Overview

## Health insurance
Northwind Corp pays 80% of the health insurance premium for the employee. Dependents can be added, and the employee pays the rest of the premium for them.

## Retirement plan
Northwind Corp offers a 401(k) retirement plan and matches your contributions up to 4% of your salary. Employees are eligible from their first day.

## Learning budget
Every employee gets a learning budget of $1,000 per year for courses, books and conferences. Unused budget does not roll over.

## Gym membership
Employees get a $30 monthly gym membership reimbursement.
