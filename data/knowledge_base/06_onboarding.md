# Onboarding Guide

## First day
On your first day you will receive your laptop and badge from IT, and meet your assigned buddy. Your buddy is an experienced colleague who answers your day-to-day questions.

## Probation period
New employees have a 90-day probation period. A review meeting with your manager takes place at the end of day 90.

## Mandatory training
All new employees must complete the security awareness training within their first 2 weeks.

## Paperwork
Return your signed contract and tax forms to HR by the end of your first week.
