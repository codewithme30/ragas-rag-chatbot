# Code of Conduct

## Respect
Northwind Corp does not tolerate harassment or discrimination of any kind. Everyone is expected to treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect.

## Reporting concerns
You can report a concern to your manager, to HR, or anonymously through the Ethics Hotline at 1-800-555-0199. Northwind Corp does not allow retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Gifts
Employees must not accept gifts worth more than $50 from customers or suppliers. Gifts above this value must be reported to Compliance.

## Confidential information
Do not share customer data or internal documents outside the company without written approval from Legal.
