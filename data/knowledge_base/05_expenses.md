# Expense Policy

## Submitting claims
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase. Claims submitted later than 30 days will be rejected.

## Receipts
A receipt is required for every expense over $25.

## Approval limits
Your manager can approve expenses up to $1,000. Anything above $1,000 must be approved by the Finance Director before you buy.

## Travel
Book flights and hotels through the company travel portal. Economy class is required for flights shorter than 6 hours. Meals while traveling are reimbursed up to $60 per day.
