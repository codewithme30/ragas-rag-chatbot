# Sick Leave Policy

## Sick days
Every employee gets 10 paid sick days per year. Sick days do not carry over to the next year.

## Doctor's note
A doctor's note is required only when you are out sick for more than 3 consecutive days. For shorter absences no note is needed.

## How to report
If you are sick, tell your manager before your normal start time, by chat or phone. You do not need to submit a request in the HR portal for sick days of 3 days or fewer.
