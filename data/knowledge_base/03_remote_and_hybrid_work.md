# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## Hybrid schedule
Most teams follow a hybrid schedule: employees work from the office 3 days per week (Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday) and may work from home on Monday and Friday.

## Fully remote work
Fully remote work is allowed only with written approval from your VP. Fully remote employees must still travel to the office for the quarterly team meetup.

## Home office stipend
Northwind Corp pays a one-time home office stipend of $500 to help you set up a desk and chair at home. Employees may claim it after their first 90 days.

## Working from another country
Working from another country for more than 14 days per year needs approval from HR because of tax and legal rules.
